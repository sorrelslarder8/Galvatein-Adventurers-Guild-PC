## 🕹️ About

Galvatein: Adventurers' Guild is a fantasy guild-reception simulation developed by BLANBEE and published by GRAVITY. As a guild receptionist, you verify completed quests by identifying monsters, examining loot, consulting bestiaries and maps, and questioning adventurers. Each chapter presents a distinct story in the continent of Eustace, combining point-and-click investigation, visual-novel storytelling, meaningful choices, and character-driven events. The game entered Early Access on September 22, 2026.

Galvatein is for players who enjoy relaxed mystery, visual novels, point-and-click investigation, fantasy storytelling, and choice-driven simulations.

## ✨ Features

- 🍲 **Fantasy Cooking** — Prepare dishes by balancing ingredients, spices, and elemental traits with help from Caul.
- 🎨 **Anime-Inspired Fantasy** — Explore a colorful 2D presentation filled with expressive characters and story-focused scenes.
- 🗺️ **Quest Verification** — Review quest details and determine whether adventurers completed their assignments correctly.
- 📖 **Omnibus Storytelling** — Experience separate chapters featuring unique events across a richly detailed fantasy world.
- ⚖️ **Meaningful Decisions** — Make judgments and choices that shape conversations, outcomes, and the stories you encounter.
- 🔍 **Evidence-Based Investigation** — Analyze loot, maps, licenses, and bestiary records to identify monsters and verify claims.
- 💬 **Adventurer Interviews** — Ask adventurers about monsters, loot, missing people, and the journeys behind their quests.
- 🌍 **The Continent of Eustace** — Meet elves, dwarves, and other adventurers carrying personal struggles and ambitions.
